# Math Platform — Privacy Policy
  
  **Last updated: May 31, 2026**
  
  Math Platform is an iPad app that helps elementary students practice
  multiplication and division using AI-graded handwriting. This policy
  explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and who
  can see it.
  
  ## Who runs the app
  
  Math Platform is operated by Valerie Sterne. For privacy questions,
  contact: valeriesterne@gmail.com.
  
  ## What information we collect
  
  When a student or teacher uses the app, we collect:
  
  - **Account information:** a username, a PIN (stored as a one-way
    cryptographic hash — we never see the plain PIN), and a display name.
  - **Student progress data:** which exercises a student has attempted,
    whether each attempt was correct, when they practiced, and how
    their mastery level is tracking over time.
  - **Handwriting input:** when a student draws an answer on the canvas,
    the drawing is recognized **on-device** using Google ML Kit Digital
    Ink Recognition. The recognized text (e.g., "24") is stored as part
    of the student's attempt record so the app can show progress and
    spaced-repetition reviews. The raw stroke data and the drawing image
    do **not** leave the device and are not sent to any cloud service.
  
  We do **not** collect: 
  
  - Email addresses (account login uses a username, not an email)
  - Phone numbers
  - Location data
  - Advertising identifiers
  - Health data
  - Contacts
  
  We do **not** track users across other apps or websites.
  
  ## Where the data is stored
  
  Student accounts, progress data, and grading history are stored in
  Supabase (https://supabase.com), a cloud database provider, on
  servers located in the United States. Handwriting recognition runs
  entirely on the iPad using on-device machine learning — drawings
  themselves never leave the device.
  
  ## Who can see the data
  
  - Valerie Sterne (the operator) can view a student's account, name,
    and progress data for the purpose of running the app and supporting
    the student's learning.
  - A student's teacher (if the student is connected to one) can view
    the student's progress.
  - Other students cannot see each other's data.
  - The data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for
    marketing.
  
  ## Children's privacy
  
  Math Platform is designed for elementary-age students. The app should
  only be used with the consent of a parent or guardian. We collect
  the minimum information needed to operate the app and provide
  personalized practice. Parents or guardians may request that we
  delete a student's account and all associated data at any time by
  emailing valeriesterne@gmail.com — we will delete the data within
  30 days. 
  
  ## Data retention
  
  We keep account and progress data as long as the account is active.
  If an account is deleted (by request or by the operator), all
  associated data — including grading history and progress records —
  is removed from our database within 30 days.
  
  ## Changes to this policy
  
  If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the
  top of this page and (if appropriate) notify users in-app.
